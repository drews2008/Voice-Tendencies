How to say it

Down, distance, hash: "third and five, middle" or "3 5 2". Hash is left, middle or right (1, 2, 3). Short means 1 to 3 yards, medium 5, long 8 or more. Also: "first play of drive, right", "first and goal", or "2 point try".
